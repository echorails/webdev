Beshbarmak

  • Time: 2–3 hours
  • Serves: 6–8 people
Ingredients Quantity
Beef or lamb 1 kg
Onions 3 large
Flour 2 cups
Eggs 2
Salt 1 tsp
Pepper 1/2 tsp
Water 1 cup

Step 1: Boil the meat in water with a pinch of salt and pepper until tender (around 2 hours).

Step 2: While the meat is boiling, slice the onions thinly and cook them in a small amount of broth from the meat until soft.

Step 3: Prepare the dough: mix flour, eggs, salt, and water until smooth, then roll it thin and cut into squares or diamonds.

Step 4: Boil the pasta squares in salted water for a few minutes until cooked. Drain and set aside.

Step 5: Slice the cooked meat into small pieces and place over the pasta. Pour the onion sauce on top.

Step 6: Serve hot, traditionally with the broth on the side.
